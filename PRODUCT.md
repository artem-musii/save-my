# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders and operations leads in small organizations of roughly 3 to 50 people. They use SAVE MY… during calm operational planning, before a launch, exam period, service rush, or high-touch customer commitment. Their primary task is to find undocumented dependencies and rehearse what fails when a person, service, vendor, device, or recovery path disappears.

## Product Purpose

SAVE MY… is a visual business-continuity workspace. A personal account contains one or more companies; each company contains an authoritative baseline map, a stored scenario library, explicit people and roles, and an activity trail. Deterministic rehearsals show what becomes blocked, while manual actions can update the baseline and browser-agent actions remain reversible proposals until a person accepts them.

## Brand Personality

Calm, severe, exact, and recognizably human. A low-chroma near-black operational shell removes distraction while a spatial graph makes causality tangible. Copy is short and direct without pretending uncertainty is resolved. The SAVE MY… ellipsis is always present in the full wordmark and is embedded in the compact symbol.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS card grids and metric dashboards.
- Default React Flow or graph-database viewer styling.
- AI chatbot shells attached to ordinary CRUD.
- Neon observability dashboards, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, shadows, and gamified resilience scores.
- Generic AI-generated-dashboard aesthetics, stock illustrations, and synthetic imagery with malformed text.
- Org charts, mind maps, and node editors that do not rehearse causal failure.

## Design Principles

1. The company is the product boundary. Keep account, company, baseline, stored scenarios, people, and activity clearly separated.
2. State provenance is never decoration. DECLARED, INFERRED, VERIFIED, UNKNOWN, STALE, and DISPUTED must remain legible and distinct.
3. Motion explains causality. It shows the initiating failure, hop-by-hop cascade, smallest affected subgraph, and restoration.
4. Determinism earns trust. The backend calculates impact; the agent structures intent and drafts reversible changes.
5. Human authority stays visible. A person may edit, delegate, or reschedule directly; an agent may only stage those changes for review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every graph state must have a structured list alternative and must not rely on color alone. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, 44px touch targets, and usable layouts from 320px through 1440px.
