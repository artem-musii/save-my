## Production graph asset pack

Generated on 2026-08-31 with the built-in image-generation tool. Original outputs remain under the Codex generated-images directory; project-bound copies are versioned below. The four atlases were deterministically cropped into 100 WebP graph-node assets under `public/assets/nodes/{sector}/01.webp` through `25.webp`. Cropping and resizing did not synthesize or alter image content.

Asset assignment is semantic, not positional. Every one of the 124 seeded demo entities has an explicit reviewed mapping in `src/infrastructure/demoWorkspaces.ts`. Agent-drafted company blueprints use separate reviewed pools by entity type. People receive portrait crops; teams receive group/operations imagery; devices, locations, documents, accounts, workflows, channels, and recovery mechanisms receive matching non-portrait imagery. Automated tests verify that every referenced file exists, no non-person uses a portrait, every seeded person has a distinct portrait within its company, and dynamic person/device/location assignments remain in their reviewed pools.

### Final generation prompts

**Retired raster exploration:** “Create a single production-ready brand mark for a business continuity and organizational resilience product named Save My. Deliver the symbol only with no words and no letters. The concept should be intentionally ambiguous: three interlocking open loops can read as a bridge, a protected path, an ellipsis, or people handing work forward. Strong memorable silhouette, rounded geometric construction, clever negative space, premium editorial software brand, not a shield, not a heart, not a chain-link cliché. Use deep ink, warm ivory, and one restrained mint accent. Flat vector-like treatment with crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, centered with generous transparent padding. Transparent background. Square 1024 × 1024.”

**Current logo:** code-native SVG in `src/web/App.tsx`. It is an open continuity bridge crossed by three small isometric blocks. The blocks form the ellipsis and share the graph node's faceted spatial construction. The full wordmark always renders as `SAVE MY…` with the single ellipsis character.

**Atlas pattern:** “Create one cohesive 5 × 5 contact-sheet asset atlas containing exactly 25 equal square tiles for a fictional small [mobile app studio / online education company / independent restaurant / charter aviation operations company] continuity map. Every tile must be a distinct useful graph-node image with no text and no logos.” Each sector prompt then enumerated its people, systems, workflows, records, devices, recovery tools, locations, and vendors. All four concluded: “Premium editorial photography mixed with restrained tactile 3D still life, realistic materials, warm ivory, deep ink, mint, coral and periwinkle accents. Clear central subject in every tile, consistent soft directional light, high detail, no captions, no letters, no recognizable brands, no watermarks. Tiles separated by clean thin gutters so they can be cropped reliably. Square 2048 × 2048.”
